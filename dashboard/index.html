OpenKnowledgeAtlas

Social network analysis of academic co-authorship, tracing who collaborates with whom across the global research system — and who is structurally left out. Each field below is analysed independently with the same method: bibliometric records from OpenAlex, institutional-country geocoding, and network/equity analysis against World Bank income classifications.
