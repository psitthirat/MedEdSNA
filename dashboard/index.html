Decolonizing Health Professions Education Research

An analysis of global co-authorship network patterns and equity implications across leading HPE journals. Bibliometric data have been retrieved with OpenAlex API and mapping with institutional country of authors to trace who collaborates with whom, and who is structurally left out.

Collaboration equity

Each publication classified as High-income only authorship, developing-country only (no high-income co-author), or mixed. The diverging axis makes the size of the gap, and how much of it is bridged by mixed-income collaboration, visible year over year.

Which income groups co-publish together

Among publications with authors from more than one country, the top 10 combinations of income groups represented, ranked by frequency — including within-group collaborations. Filled dots mark the income groups present in that combination.

Centrality & participation distributions

Who is rising and falling in the network

Rank of each country by centrality measure, every year from 2015 to 2026 in the cumulative network. Labels on the right are the current top 10; labels on the left were top 10 in 2015 but have since fallen out of it.
